# Stance: product

**Purpose:** turn observable product truth into a persuasive product story.
**Voice:** direct, credible, benefit-led and respectful of the viewer's time.
**Lead with it when** the truth depends on an observable product, website, application, recording or user journey, and the video must prove a promise through what the product visibly does.
**Default for:** `screen-demo`. A product teaser in `animated-explainer`, `animation` or `cinematic` can be led with this stance too.

## Priorities

1. First-party product evidence: the real product, its real states and results.
2. A coherent user journey: one workflow, one outcome, in the order a user lives it.
3. Benefit-led storytelling: what the viewer gains, shown, not a feature list.
4. Interface readability: every piece of text the viewer needs is legible at the delivery size.
5. A clear call to action: the one thing to do next.

## Questions to ask

- Which user problem can the product visibly resolve?
- Which product states prove the central promise?
- What is the single result moment, and is it shown at real speed?
- What should the viewer do immediately after watching?

## Avoid

- Fabricated product behaviour, or claims the product cannot be shown making good on.
- Unreadable capture: tiny text, extreme crops, low-bitrate encodes.
- Feature-list narration that describes the cursor instead of the outcome.
- A synthetic or rebuilt interface presented as real capture; label it as illustration.
- Exposed keys, tokens, emails or personal data in any frame.

## Quality bar

- Interface text is readable at the output size; no crop smaller than half the source resolution.
- Attention cues are sparse: at most two at once, each appearing just before its action and never covering what it points at.
- Dead time (loading, installs, typing) is cut or sped up and stays under 10% of the output; the proof plays at normal speed.
- Narration matches what is on screen, step by step.
- Sensitive data is masked wherever it appears.
- Titles and metadata name the real tool, the task and the outcome.
