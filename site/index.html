A video studio for the agent you already use.

Facet gives Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot CLI, OpenCode and Compa a studio's way of working. Your agent understands the brief, researches, proposes concepts, writes and tests the story and plans every scene before it makes anything. Then it narrates, composes in Remotion, HyperFrames or FFmpeg, captions, mixes and reviews the result.

Install Facet @VERSION@ Source on GitHub

Free and open source, for Windows, macOS and Linux on x64 and Arm64. Facet never calls a model: your agent does.

One studio, three ways in.

The facet command

35 tools that probe, edit, narrate, compose, caption, generate and review video; 12 production pipelines with their stage guides; and an MCP server that offers them to your agent. People and scripts run the same tools directly.

Facet in your agent

facet wire adds Facet's guide and MCP server to Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot CLI, OpenCode or Compa, its agents where the CLI has them, and an ask before each tool that may charge. Claude Code, Codex and Copilot CLI can also add Facet from its plugin marketplace.

The Facet App

Facet in its own window, a page in your browser, for people who don't work in a terminal agent. It runs its own agent with a model you connect, keeps your projects and their media, and asks in the window before any tool that may charge.

Thinking first, then making.

Every video goes through a pipeline chosen by how it is made: an animated explainer, a kinetic animation, a cinematic piece, a documentary montage from stock, an edit of your own footage. Your agent is the producer; separate agents research and review. Each step writes a record you can read and change.

  1. Brief

    The job, the audience, the length and what you will spend.

  2. Research

    Sources, data and real questions, gathered by a researcher agent.

  3. Concepts

    Genuinely different directions, each grounded in the research and buildable with what you have.

    You choose

  4. Story

    The script, checked against the length and the hook, and reviewed by a critic agent.

    You approve

  5. Scene plan

    Every scene and how it moves, checked for gaps, variety and slideshow risk.

    You approve

  6. Production

    Narration, media, music, the edit and the render, sampled before any batch.

    You approve the samples

  7. Review and delivery

    The critic and output_review check the video against what was promised, and every source and paid call is listed.

    You accept

One command, for your account only.

No administrator rights. The installer checks every file against the release's SHA-256 checksums, then asks which components to install.

The App, with the facet command

Windows, in PowerShell

irm https://kayiik.github.io/facet/install.ps1 | iex

macOS and Linux

curl -fsSL https://kayiik.github.io/facet/install.sh | sh

The App starts and opens in your browser. Later, start it from the Start menu, your Applications folder or your application menu.

Facet for your agent

Windows, in PowerShell

& ([scriptblock]::Create((irm https://kayiik.github.io/facet/install.ps1))) -Mode bundle -Harness claude,opencode

macOS and Linux

curl -fsSL https://kayiik.github.io/facet/install.sh | sh -s -- --mode bundle --harness claude,opencode

Name your CLIs: claude, codex, copilot, opencode or compa. Then start a new session, choose the facet agent where your CLI has one, and ask for a video.

On macOS and Linux, facet is linked into ~/.local/bin, which must be on your PATH. Run the installer again to upgrade, verify or remove Facet. Its programs are not code-signed, so Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: the checksums show that the files are intact, not who published them. Every option

The programs it runs on, installed beside it.

Each one is pinned and checked against its SHA-256 before it is unpacked. A supported copy already on your computer is used instead.

The first five are installed by default. facet components shows what is installed, on this computer or missing; change it at any time:

facet components
facet install piper
facet remove hyperframes

Your work stays yours. Spending is your call.

Facet runs on your computer and calls no model. Your agent, and the model it uses, stay exactly as they are.

  • Facet sends no telemetry. It goes online only for what you ask of it: the free Edge voices, stock media searches, the components it installs, and paid providers when you agree.
  • Eight tools may charge, through OpenAI, ElevenLabs, fal.ai and Google Flow. Your agent announces each paid call and whose credits it uses, and your CLI asks you before it runs.
  • A complete free path always exists: an Edge or Piper voice, stock media, Remotion scenes and your own music.
  • Provider keys stay in your environment. Facet reads a key only to call its provider.
  • The App listens only on 127.0.0.1, and answers only the window it opened, with a key it makes at each start.
